Privacy Policy for Portfolio Pulse - Net Worth

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Portfolio Pulse ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal finance tracking application developed for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or financial data with us or with any third party. We do not operate any server or backend that receives your data.

2. Local Storage Only

All information you enter into the App — including income and expense records, transaction details, reports, and any UPI payment-related data — is stored exclusively on your device, in a local database. This data never leaves your device and is not synced to any cloud service, server, or external storage operated by us.

3. No Access to Your Data

Because all data resides only on your device, we have no access to your financial records, transaction history, or any other information you enter into the App. We cannot view, retrieve, back up, or recover this data on your behalf.

4. UPI Payment Integration

The App may launch third-party UPI payment apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or similar) installed on your device to facilitate payments. When you use this feature:

  • The App only passes standard UPI payment parameters (such as payee VPA, amount, and reference note) to the payment app you select.
  • The App does not process, store, or have access to your bank account details, UPI PIN, or transaction credentials — these are handled entirely by the third-party payment app and your bank/payment service provider.
  • Any data handling by third-party payment apps is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

5. Camera Permission (QR Scanning)

The App may request camera access to scan UPI QR codes for the purpose of pre-filling payment details. Camera access is used only at the moment of scanning and no images or video are stored, recorded, or transmitted anywhere.

6. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing the App's data/storage via Android Settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App, which permanently removes all associated local data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, since it does not collect data at all. The App is not specifically directed at children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
