Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Portfolio Pulse ("the app") handles your information.

Information we access

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile picture through Google Sign-In. We use this only to identify your account inside the app.

The app may also use your device camera to scan UPI QR codes. Camera images are processed on your device and are not stored or uploaded.

How we use it

Your information is used only to provide the app's features, such as signing you in and showing your own data. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for advertising.

Payments

UPI payments are completed through the UPI app you choose on your device. The app does not store your bank account details, UPI PIN or card information.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on your device and/or in secure services used by the app. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage is completely secure.

Deleting your data

You can uninstall the app at any time to remove its local data. To request deletion of any data associated with your account, contact us at the email below.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
