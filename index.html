About the app

Portfolio Pulse is an Android app that helps you keep track of your assets and personal finances and make UPI payments by scanning QR codes.

Features

  • Track and manage your expenses and income streams
  • Track and manage your assets in one place
  • Scan UPI QR codes to pay
  • See income expense and asset reports
  • Sign in securely with your Google account
